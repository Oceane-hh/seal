How it works

One table. Six states. Three exits.

01IntentFriday 19:30, two, 30th birthday. Mandate $80.
02Offer19:45 two-top. Unsigned.
03Counter$40 hold. Seated by 20:00. Signed.
04Seal$40 authorized on the card. Not captured.
05HonorSeated. Authorization released.
06BreachNo-show. $40 captured per Counter.

Honor releases. Breach captures. Void reverses the authorization if the house cannot perform or the Intent exceeded the mandate. Funds never sit in a SEAL wallet.

01

Mandate

What the principal will allow: covers, window, maximum authorization. The merchant cannot collect above it.

02

Counter

One signed set of merchant terms. Matching is two agents, not a first-come calendar. Settlement executes that Counter only.

03

Settle

Delayed capture on existing card rails. Not a wallet. Performance releases. Non-performance captures. Invalid terms void.

Position

Authorize now. Capture only if the Counter is breached.

Identity names the parties. Card rails move the funds. SEAL sits between: a mandate, a signed Counter, and an authorization that does not capture until those terms resolve. We do not compete with the rails.
